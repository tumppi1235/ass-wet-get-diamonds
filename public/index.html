How the odds work +

New surroundings influence an artefact’s rarity when first revealed. Its rarity then stays fixed.

Distance since collection builds search energy. Rare finds start at 30%, rising to 85% at 8 km. Weather adds 5–10 percentage points; outing distance adds up to 5. Final odds cap at 90%.

Success spends 2 km of energy. A miss preserves it. One attempt per tile per outing. Collected artefacts stay collected. No rewards for speed or extreme weather.

Visits are your own, not public footfall. Weather is manual, not a live feed. This is a gameplay prototype, not Garmin firmware or the Connect IQ emulator.

    YOUR FIRST EXPEDITION

    A town full of small mysteries.

    1. Load the city demo or click the map to plot a route. Press Start route to move. You can also use WASD or arrow keys.

    2. Explore 250 m tiles to reveal artefacts in your tile and its eight neighbours. Switch the watch between Guidance and Nearby map. Unfamiliar surroundings have better rarity odds.

    3. Tap the watch screen, click its side key, or press Enter / E. One action stops simulated movement and attempts collection. No countdown or extra confirmation.

    4. Travel farther to improve your collection odds. Try rain or snow to see weather bonuses and themed artefacts.

    Failed searches preserve energy. Start a new outing to retry; your explored tiles and collection remain. Progress is saved on this browser.

    The city map is real; the movement, weather and treasures are simulated. This app does not run on your Garmin yet.
